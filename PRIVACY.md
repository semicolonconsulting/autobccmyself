# Privacy policy

Auto BCC Myself for Gmail does its work entirely in your browser. It doesn't collect, send or share any data.

## What it reads

On `mail.google.com` only, it reads:

- The tab title, to find the address of the Gmail account you're signed in to.
- The recipient fields of compose windows, to check whether you're already a recipient and to add your address to Bcc.

It doesn't read your messages, contacts or anything on other websites.

## What it stores

Two settings, saved in the browser's local extension storage on your computer:

- whether the add-on is switched on, and
- the Bcc address you set in the popup, if any.

They aren't synced or sent anywhere. Removing the add-on deletes them.

## What it sends

Nothing. The add-on makes no network requests and has no analytics or tracking. The Bcc copy of each message is sent by Gmail, to your own address, as part of the message you send.

## Permissions

- **Access to `mail.google.com`**: to add the Bcc in Gmail's compose window.
- **`storage`**: to save the two settings above.
